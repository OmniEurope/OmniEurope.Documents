JPEG 2000 conformance samples

They are test data only: never shipped in the package.

1. Public test inputs. Every file under Samples/ not listed in 2 and 3 is taken byte for byte (with its name
   shortened) from https://github.com/SerenityOS/serenity, directory Tests/LibGfx/test-inputs/jpeg2000/
   (branch master read on 2026-10-09; every file identical to its blob at commit 2267e6acbcc3, the last change to
   that directory).

2. Rewritten public test inputs. packed-ppm.jp2, packed-ppt.jp2, poc-main.jp2, poc-tile.jp2, offset-origin.jp2 and
   signed-gray.jp2 hold the coded data of the inputs of 1 (lossless-rgba-u8-prog0-EPH-SOP.jp2,
   lossless-rgba-u8-prog2-tile4x2-cblk4x16-tp3-layers3-res2.jp2, lossless-gray-u8-prog1-layers1-res6.jp2) with their
   markers rearranged: packet headers moved to PPM or PPT markers, packets reordered under a POC marker, the image
   and tile origins moved by a multiple of every code-block and precinct size, components declared signed.

3. Samples made for this repository, under its licence (EUPL-1.2): the files whose name ends in -a or -b,
   subsampled-420-cprl.j2k, packed-ppm-tiles.jp2, packed-ppt-tiles.jp2 and tile-cod.jp2 under Samples/, and
   Large/large-4096x3072.jp2. They were encoded by two independent JPEG 2000 encoders (-a and -b) from synthetic
   test patterns computed for the purpose; the last three rewrite tiles-sop-eph-b.jp2 as 2 does. The large image
   encodes the pattern the stress test computes again (tests/Shared/Jpeg2000Conformance.cs, Pattern).

The PGM files under Expected/ are reference planes derived from the samples (see manifest.txt).
