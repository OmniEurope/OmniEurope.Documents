JBIG2 conformance samples

The .jbig2 files under Samples/ and Large/ are public test inputs, taken unchanged from
https://github.com/SerenityOS/serenity, directory Tests/LibGfx/test-inputs/jbig2/ (commit 6dfb6940a682,
2026-09-12). They are test data only: never shipped in the package. annex-h.jbig2 holds the test data
sequence of ITU-T Recommendation T.88, annex H.

The PBM files under Expected/ are reference bitmaps derived from those samples (see manifest.txt).
