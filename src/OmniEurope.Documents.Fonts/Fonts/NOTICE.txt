OmniEurope.Documents.Fonts - third-party fonts

Every font below is shipped unmodified (only the file names of Selawik are changed, from selawk*.ttf to
Selawik-<Style>.ttf). Each one is embedded in OmniEurope.Documents.Fonts.dll with its licence file, and the
licence files are also shipped in the NuGet package under fonts/extra/. A PDF produced by the library embeds a
subset of the glyphs used, as the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (the licence of every font here) allows.
